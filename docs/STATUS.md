# Implementation status

The directory is served by Cloudflare Workers at https://madagascar-ngo-directory.mada-tools.workers.dev/. The renamed address was verified on 2026-11-19. A custom domain and canonical URL are deferred to handoff.

- The original 28 seed rows are reconciled. EDENa is now listed for sourced work in Analamanga and Alaotra-Mangoro. NORA Madagascar and the EDENa lead in Ambatosoa still need local programme evidence.
- The national pass brings the directory to 77 profiles, including 74 NGOs and nonprofits and three public coordination listings. Each of Madagascar's 24 regions has at least three organisations in the default search, with results for agriculture, education and health in each region. The count includes programmes whose current status needs confirmation.
- A deeper Analamanga pass brings the directory to 110 profiles and the default Analamanga results from 8 to 44. The research used the France Volontaires 2025 host list as one lead alongside programme and partner sources. Each addition links to local activity and a public contact route. Separately documented programmes also improved coverage in several other regions.
- A second research pass covers the other 23 regions and brings the directory to 148 profiles. It draws on programme reports, regional directories, farmer networks and public social posts with identifiable programmes. Default results grew in 22 regions and gained an additional agriculture lead in Ambatosoa. Four region and priority-sector combinations still have a single lead: education and health in Ambatosoa, and health in Betsiboka and Bongolava. Partner referrals and past projects are labelled on the relevant records.
- The [coverage tracker](coverage.csv) links to the sources and records outstanding checks for each region and sector. The contribution page has a confirmation template for volunteers to report which programmes and public contact routes they have checked. Programme and contact confirmations are tracked separately.
